## Description (both stores, 4,000 characters at most)

DSIP is a phone and messenger where your identity is yours. It is a key on your device, not an account on someone's
server, and it is the open DSIP protocol underneath, so no single company stands between you and the people you talk
to.

Calls
- Call anyone by their identity, on the relay of your choice.
- On a phone the system's call screen rings, on the lock screen too, and a sleeping phone is woken for a call.
- Earpiece, speaker, Bluetooth and wired headsets.

Messages
- One-to-one and group conversations, encrypted end to end with MLS.
- People reach you only once you accept their introduction: no unsolicited messages.
- Your mailbox holds messages while you are offline, and cannot read them.

Presence
- Share whether you are available with the people you choose. It is sealed, so the relay passes it on without reading
  it.

Your identity, your keys
- Your keys stay in your device's keystore (the Keychain, the Android Keystore, Credential Manager, the Secret
  Service).
- Back your identity up as a recovery code under a passphrase, and restore it on a new device.
- Add your other phones and computers by pairing. They get their own keys, and never your identity's.
- Use a domain you own as your identity (did:web), and change keys without losing it.

Open
- The protocol, its conformance suite and two independent implementations are public.
- No ads, no analytics, no tracking. The app sends what a call or a message needs, to the services you choose:
  see the privacy policy.

Accessible
- Screen readers, large text, keyboard use on desktop, and contrast that meets WCAG AA.
